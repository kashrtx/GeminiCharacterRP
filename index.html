Phone & Netlify Local AI

Desktop browsers can use localhost directly. Phones and the Netlify HTTPS app need a bridge so the browser can reach Ollama or LM Studio on your PC.

  1. Install Tampermonkey in the browser that opens this app.
  2. Install the Local AI Bridge userscript.
  3. Keep your phone and PC on the same Wi-Fi.
  4. Start Ollama or LM Studio with local-network access enabled on the PC.
  5. Use your PC LAN IP endpoint, such as http://192.168.1.25:11434 for Ollama or http://192.168.1.25:1234/v1 for LM Studio.
  6. Click Test Configuration before chatting.
